# Parts v2 icons (for the art session)

Generated by `docs/design/parts-v2/gen_icons.py` from `docs/design/parts-v2/data.py`. **150 icons: one per part.** Icon key = the game item id (file name).

## Drawing rules

- 512x512, transparent, one object, centred, ~80% fill. The client draws it on a 170x170 rarity tile (at 1080 width) with ~12% padding: keep a margin. No frame, background, ground, shadow or text.
- Style of the approved icons: bold black outline, flat colours, two-tone cel shading.
- Parts are robot/sci-fi gear for a cartoon space worm: helm (head), chest plate (body), tail segment (tail), pair of leg units (legs), a weapon arm (arm: hammer, cannon, whip, claw, drill or blaster on a short mechanical arm socket; arms are interchangeable, the weapon is the look).
- **Tier progression must read at a glance** within one slot+role family (same silhouette idea, rising quality): Scrap (Common) crude scrap → Alloy (Rare) clean metal → Plasma (Epic) glowing seams + core → Quantum (Legendary) ornate, crystal inlays, floating segments. Mythics are unique hero items with their element front and centre.
- Role colour and motif are constant across the 4 tiers of a family so they read as one line of gear.
