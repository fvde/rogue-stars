Decided in review round 1 (2026-11-28)

  • No families: There are only biomes. Each biome has its own roster (enemies, elites, boss); enemies shared between biomes are simply listed in each. The level order only avoids the same biome twice in a row.
  • Difficulty: Comes only from the level scaling (threat curve, early ramp, chapter scale). No biome is harder or easier as a whole; the old per-family offsets go.
  • Surge: The Biomass surge drops from ×4 to ×2 in general. A Biomass biome on a surge run pays ×4 (×2 biome × ×2 surge).
  • Invasion bosses: Rust Queen, Gloop Matriarch, Hollow Cantor and Prism Tyrant double as biome bosses; invasions keep them.
  • Unlock path: Signatures unlock by campaign level (no Laboratory packages). Each unlock brings one new signature and one new biome into the rotation: 4 biomes at the start, +1 at levels 10, 20, 35, 60, 90 and so on.
  • Effects: Keep every effect simple: one thing, no new game mechanic or asset. Boss abilities, events and Rites below use only effects the game already has.
  • Laboratory: The six signature packages go with no replacement; the tree is restructured around the gap.
  • Rites: Biome events may know the biome's enemies and boss. Each biome gets a Rite: an event whose choices change this level's enemies or boss (owner example: give up an Ice skill in Lava Fields, Fire enemies are weakened for the run).

How every event option shows its result

Per the event-outcome rework, each option below is tagged:

  • R · sheet random: the outcome is news, so the result sheet opens (chance bar sweeps, SUCCESS / NO LUCK, one line per gain or loss).
  • D · fly-in deterministic: the button already says what happens; no sheet, the gains fly into the HUD (coins, HP, stat chips, chest to the pending bag). A fight starts the battle directly.
  • toast nothing: "You move on." as a short toast.

Texts follow the house rules: trigger → effect → number, round numbers, one gain and at most one price per option. "X chest (paid on clear, +Danger)" is account loot and counts toward the 3 account-loot offers per run (RunTuning.MaxAccountLootOffersPerRun).

Every option uses an existing RunEffectKind (Coins, HealPct, DamagePct, StatPercent, GainSkill, Battle, PendingReward, BossStatPct, Meter, AwakenRatePct, StrengthenEnemies, Shards, Xp). A biome chest is a PendingReward of one resource. The only new piece is the Rite Offering (pick an owned skill of one synergy to remove), which reuses the quest sacrifice.

Synergy weather

Every biome boosts one synergy and weakens another (owner addition). It tells the player at a glance which build shines here, and the boosted synergy is usually the one the biome's enemies use, so the boost cuts both ways.

Rule

  • What it scales: the damage of that synergy only. Heat / Supernova: Fire damage and the Heat burst. Chill / Freeze: Ice damage. Shock / Arc: Lightning damage. Radiation: Radiation damage (DoT). Bleed: Bleed damage. Combo: combo hits. Counter: counter hits. Alien: alien ability damage. Time: Time skill damage. General: damage of skills with no synergy tag. Chances, stack caps, durations and Freeze / Delay / Stun turns stay as they are, so the text can stay one number.
  • Numbers: boost +25% everywhere. Weakened −25%, or −50% for a direct opposite (Chill in Lava Fields and Desert Ruins, Heat on the Seabed, Bleed in Clockwork City).
  • Who: the weakened synergy hits the player only, so a biome never feels easier because its own enemy status got cut. The boost also hits the enemies that use that synergy (named on each card), e.g. Ember enemies' Heat in Lava Fields. Bosses count as enemies.
  • Stacking (recommended): a separate final multiplier (×1.25 or ×0.75 / ×0.5) applied after all additive bonuses from skills, parts, aliens, heroes and the Laboratory. Example: +40% Fire damage from skills and parts → ×1.4, then Lava ×1.25 → ×1.75. It can never go below zero, so no floor is needed. Alternative: add it to the same pool (+40% + 25% = +65%) with a floor of 25% of base damage; this is harder to read when the weakened synergy is the player's main build.
  • Shown: two chips on the portal card and the level-select portal (“Heat +25%” green, “Chill −50%” red), a banner at level start, and a small “+25% here” / “−50% here” badge on signature cards, skill drafts and shop skills whose tags match. In battle the damage numbers include it; the battle HUD keeps the two chips in a corner.
  • Signature pick: the level-start banner shows before the signature choice (from level 3), and the two signature cards carry the badge, so the player can lean in or hedge.
  • Modes: main campaign (Normal and Overcharged) only. Dark Side (ash wastes, already +45–90% enemy stats) and Burrow floors get no weather; a rotating Dark Side weather is a possible later twist. Invasions and the Arena: none.

Rules

  • Thresholds: 10, 20, 35, 60, 90 as given, then spread to 500: 125, 160, 200, 245, 295, 350, 420, 500 (gaps grow from 35 to 80). Today's ceiling with perfect gear is about level 300, so the last 3 biomes wait for future power; that is intended.
  • First visit: the level right after an unlock is always the new biome (a “New biome” banner on its portal), then it joins the shuffle.
  • Shuffle: the seeded order shuffles the unlocked biomes in blocks of the rotation size; never the same biome twice in a row.
  • Planned biomes join when their art is in. If a planned biome is not ready at its level, its slot waits and the next ready one fills it; the signature unlocks on time regardless.
  • Laboratory: the six signature packages (Combo … Time) go, no replacement. The tree is restructured around the gap: the synergy skill nodes (Chain Strikes, Frost Nova, Overload, Knockout, Bulwark, Pack Tactics) hang off the neighbouring stat nodes and need the synergy's level unlock instead of the package. Combo moves into the start set: 4 start synergies for 4 start biomes.
  • Weather early on: with 4 start biomes that each boost a start synergy, weather can be on from level 1; the first penalty that bites a start synergy is the Seabed at level 35 (Heat −50%).

Rites: events that know the biome

Every biome has one Rite: an event that reaches into this level's enemies and boss. It plays against the weather: the biome's weakened synergy is what the land asks for.

How a Rite works

  • Offering Offering: give up one owned run skill of the biome's weakened synergy (you pick which). The land answers with one thing for the rest of the level: boss −20% ATK, boss −20% HP, the boss awakens 25% slower, or nearby enemies −25%. Shown only if you own such a skill; the skill is gone (its levels too, like the existing sacrifice). A signature can't be offered.
  • Second option: a biome-specific deal with one gain and one price (HP, coins, or a stronger boss).
  • Leave is always there.
  • Placement (decided): the Rite is the 5th biome event, with double weight, and only on event tiles in the first half of the board, so its effect still has a boss to matter for. About 1 visit in 3 meets it. Cost: the offered skill, nothing more.
  • Shown: like every boss modifier today (boss ATK / HP %, awakening speed); no new art.

Rules of play

Biome reward (×2)

  • What doubles: every amount of the biome's resource that level pays: its completion line, its share of the 3 completion loot rolls and of every reward chest. Nothing else changes.
  • Never a zero ×2: Grain, Fuel, Hero XP and Gear Cores are only loot rolls today, and main levels pay no Data at all. A biome with such a resource adds one guaranteed roll of it to the completion, then doubles it. Data gets a new roll entry for its biomes (Data 10–20 × chapter scale).
  • Biome chest (events): one doubled roll of the resource, e.g. at chapter 1 Fuel 20–40, Ore 40–80; scales with the chapter like all loot, rounded (RunSystem.NiceAmount).
  • Shown: portal card chip “×2 Ore” next to the biome name; level select portal shows the same chip; result screen line “Ore 100 (×2 biome)”.
  • Never doubled by the biome: Diamonds, Crates, Signals, Schematics, milestone and bonus chests, the first-clear part, feat Diamonds.

With chapter rewards and the Biomass surge

  • Chapters keep the rhythm: reward scale, Crate every 5th step, milestone chest every 10th level, stamina, dice. The biome only picks which resource is doubled.
  • Biomass surge (first 3 won runs a day): ×2 instead of ×4 (decided). It multiplies after the biome, so a Biomass biome on a surge run pays ×4. This halves today's surge Biomass on non-Biomass biomes: the simulator run below has to re-check Biomass pacing, and the lost surge income may need to move into base Biomass.
  • Overcharged ×1.8, run-reward modifiers (planetary events) and the double-reward ad apply on top, as today.
  • Economy impact: a resource in k biomes gains ~k/17 of its per-level income (Biomass, Ore, Fuel +18%; the rest +12%), plus the guaranteed rolls. Before shipping: one simulator run (tools/RogueStars.Sim, runs-per-upgrade table in economy-model.md).

Bosses

  • One boss per biome. Biomes with 2 variants (Lava Fields, Ice Shelf, Shipwreck) alternate per visit: the biome's 1st, 3rd, 5th… appearance in the seeded order uses A, the 2nd, 4th… B. Deterministic, same for every player.
  • From level 501 the existing endless titles (Elder, Hollow, Radiant, Ashen, Ascended, Feral) and boss-tile combos keep rotating every 5 chapters on top of the biome boss.
  • NEW bosses sit on the standard boss budget (HP × ATK within ~5%), get the standard awakening actions (25/50/75/100) and 3 boss tiles named for them (feed / relief / tribute, the same numbers for every boss). From level 501 the endless boss variants borrow another boss's tiles instead of another family's.
  • Rust Queen, Gloop Matriarch, Hollow Cantor and Prism Tyrant are invasion bosses today; as biome bosses they get a board kit (awakening, tiles, escort). Invasions keep using them (decided).

Difficulty

  • Stays keyed by the level number: threat curve, early-level ramp, chapter scale, level-unlocked run content (signature pick, minibosses, forks, campfires).
  • No biome offsets: difficulty comes only from the level scaling. The current family offsets (Void −25%, Glacier −20% from level 401) go; those enemies are retuned to the standard role budget instead.
  • Level 1 stays Lava Fields (tutorial pool). Adding biomes reshuffles the order for everyone; pre-release, no migration.
  • Biome hazards are reskins of the 3 existing hazards (same numbers): only their name and tile art change.
