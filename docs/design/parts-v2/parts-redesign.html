Design only; no game code changed. Every row and card has a stable id (shown as #id) for per-item comments.

1. The model

  • Rarities. Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary are four separate item sets: per slot (head, body, tail, legs, arm; an arm part fits either arm) six items each, one per standard role: Guard (tank), Strike (offense), Swift (speed), Keen (crit), Mend (sustain), Fury (rage). Uncommon is retired. Mythic is a fifth set of 30 build enablers.
  • Obtain. Every rarity comes mainly from Parts pulls (the Shop's Parts section, paid with Crates) at the published rates: Common 58.3%, Rare 28%, Epic 9.5%, Legendary 3%, Mythic 1.2% (pity 100 / 40, every 10× has an Epic). Minor direct sources (decided): campaign boss first clears give one Rare or Epic part, Dark Side clears a 5% Legendary (see Sources).
  • Skills. Common and Rare: stats only. Epic: exactly 1 skill. Legendary: exactly 2. Mythic: 5 skills, 3 active at Mythic, the 4th at Mythic S, the 5th at Mythic SS. Awakening upgrades these five; it never adds one.
  • Stats. Slot core (head HP; body HP + DEF; tail HP + ATK; legs DEF + speed; arm ATK) × rarity (1.0 / 1.7 / 2.4 / 3.6 / 5.6) plus one role stat (Guard arms also add counter chance, Swift arms combo chance; synergy stats are fine on standard parts where the role fits, their skills stay simple); a Mythic carries its synergy stat instead, as a percentage (+10% fire / ice / Arc / radiation / bleed / Echo damage, +10% alien power, +5% combo / counter / crit chance, +10% HP, +10% rage gain), growing per level like every stat. Arms are interchangeable: any arm part fits either arm, weapon names (Hammer, Cannon, Whip …) are cosmetic, and there are no attack-style multipliers. Level caps: Common 10, Rare 20, Epic 30, Legendary 50; a Mythic's cap rises with its ladder step: Mythic 70, +1 75, +2 80, S 85, S+1 90, S+2 95, SS 100 (final; no plate raises). Levelling grows every stat: flat stats (HP, ATK, DEF, speed) add 15% of their level-1 value per level, whole numbers (Firebrand Blaster +85 ATK, +13 per level → +1 372 ATK at 100); every percentage stat (a Mythic's synergy %, HP %, rage gain; combo / counter / crit chance; life steal and the other role %) grows linearly from its level-1 value to 3× at the part's max level (Firebrand Blaster +10% fire damage → +30% at 100, awakened ×1.4: +14% → +42%; +5% crit chance → +15%; a Common +3% → +9% at its cap 10). The tables show level 1, the growth and the value at max level. Same rule for every rarity and role; awakening multiplies level-1 values ×1.4.
  • Merge. Nothing merges into a higher rarity: a Legendary never becomes a Mythic, and standard parts have no merge of their own. Only Mythics climb: Mythic → +1 → +2 → S → S+1 → S+2 → SS (final). Pulls drive it: each step up to S takes 1 copy of the same Mythic; the steps from S to SS take copies + other Mythics of the same slot: 2 + 2 (S+1), 2 + 2 (S+2), 5 + 5 (SS). Spare standard parts become Metal (Common 1, Rare 2, Epic 6, Legendary 20), a small cost on every step. Full rule in Ladder and Metal.
  • Mythic ladder. Skills 1–3 at Mythic, skill 4 at S, skill 5 at SS; the other steps give stats and level cap. Steps never grow skill numbers; bigger numbers come only from awakening.
  • Awakening (once, Mythic or higher, 50 Awaken Cores): a new name, ×1.4 base stats, and each of the five skills becomes its awakened version: the same trigger and mechanic with a bigger number, a lower threshold or a twist on the same effect. The rung still decides how many are active (3, the 4th at S, the 5th at SS). No sixth skill, no twin families, and an awakened skill is crowd control only where its base skill already is (same rung, still once per enemy).
  • Interplay. A part never sets a game value absolutely: it shifts it ("2 Heat sooner", "25 less rage"), all sources add up, and every shifted value has a floor or ceiling (Supernova ≥ 6 Heat, rage attack ≥ 50 rage, sidekick action ≥ 50 charge, dodge ≤ 85%). Blow-ups are capped across all sources (full avoidance 1 per round, extra turns 2 per round, free rage attacks 2 per turn, status ticks 3 per enemy per round). Details in Interplay with skills.
  • Crowd control exists only on Mythic S / SS skills, at most once per enemy per battle; bosses lose 20 rage instead (as the Chrono Skiff).
  • Text rule. One sentence, at most 15 words, trigger → effect → number, whole numbers (multiples of 5 from 20 up), no vague verbs, one thing per skill. Legendary and Mythic skills are mechanics, never a plain "+X%".

Mythic ladder and Metal

  • Only Mythics climb, and pulls drive the climb. Common, Rare, Epic and Legendary parts never merge into a higher rarity and have no steps of their own. A Mythic climbs Mythic → +1 → +2 → S → S+1 → S+2 → SS; SS is the final state.
  • Mythic → S: 1 copy of the same Mythic per step (+1, +2, S): 3 copies, no other Mythics. S → SS: copies + other Mythics of the same slot: 2 + 2 at S+1, 2 + 2 at S+2, 5 + 5 at SS (decided): 9 + 9. To SS: 12 copies + 9 other Mythics (13 of that Mythic counting the one you climb).
  • A copy is the same Mythic (same item) at its base rung: not climbed and not awakened. Its level Ore and runes come back.
  • An other Mythic of the same slot is any different Mythic item of that slot at its base rung, not awakened (the two arm slots share one pool: every arm Mythic is an arm). Climbed or awakened Mythics are never material. Each Mythic fills one seat only: an extra copy of the item you climb counts as a copy, never also as one of its other Mythics; for a different Mythic of that slot (e.g. your second arm) it is an other Mythic.
  • Replicator: 3 spare Mythics (any slot, base rung) + 5 000 Ore → 1 copy of a Mythic you own. With 12 copies per item this is the main copy source after the item itself (see Pacing: without it the endgame takes over twice as long).
  • Metal is the value of a spare standard part: Common 1, Rare 2, Epic 6, Legendary 20 (any slot, any role). Feeding a part turns it into Metal at once; its level Ore and runes come back. Equipped, locked and Forged parts are never fed. Mythics are never Metal: spare Mythics are ladder material or Replicator input.
  • Metal is secondary. Every step costs a little Metal (1 800 per Mythic to SS, 10 800 for six Mythics). At the effective pull rates the standard rarities bring about the same Metal per 100 pulls (Common 56, Rare 54, Epic 68, Legendary 76), 2.5 per pull. Six Mythics at SS use about a third of the Metal a player earns on the way there, and in the simulation Metal never delays a step by more than the run-to-run noise. The rest piles up: a known surplus, accepted for now (owner: no second sink).

Interplay with skills

Every part text was compared with the game's own skills and kits: all run and laboratory skills at every level, the nine signatures and their awakened levels, the run Mythics, and every hero and mount kit line (403 sentences, read from run-skill-levels.md and heroes-mounts.md, generated from ContentDb), and with the engine's values (RunDefs combat tuning, ContentDb.Run statuses, BattleSimulator rules). check.py now repeats the scan on every build.

Engine values the parts build on

  • Heat: max 10, Supernova at 10 (Controlled Burn sets 12; Flashpoint forces it at 6+). Chill: max 10, 3 turns, cools Heat; Deep Freeze freezes at 5 Chill and resets it. Shock: max 5, 2 turns; Capacitor discharges and resets at 5. Bleed: max 10 (Hemorrhage +5 / +8). Deep Wells (Prism Alchemist 10★) doubles the Bleed, Chill and Shock max.
  • Freeze and Stun: 1 turn, then 3 rounds before the same enemy can get them again. Rage: 100 for a rage attack, overfill to 125, +25 per action, +10 per hit taken. Sidekicks act at 100 charge (start at 50). Dodge at most 75% (soft knee at 10), crit at most 100%, combo chance at most 95% (80% with Endless Chain).

How parts stack with skills

  • Relative, never absolute. A part shifts a value; it never sets it. A skill that sets a value (Controlled Burn: "Supernova needs 12 Heat") sets the base, then every shift from parts, skills and kits adds up.
  • Floors and ceilings (all sources together; decided by the owner): Supernova never below 6 Heat (10 − 4 at most from parts; 12 − 4 = 8 with Controlled Burn); a rage attack never below 50 rage (Rage Turbine −25 / −40, Quantum Fury Shell −20); a sidekick action never below 50 charge; dodge never above 85% (Frostglide Skates +5 / +10 against chilled attackers is the only part that lifts the 75% cap); counter and crit chance at most 100%.
  • Conversions (Flicker Treads dodge → combo, Perfect Form combo → counter, Deadeye Visor crit → crit damage) read the stat before conversions and are never chained: a converted value is not converted again.
  • Rules that never add: executes (Cull, Exsanguinate, Deadeye Visor, Decay Stinger) use the highest threshold, never the sum; area basic attacks (Thunderhead 60%, Quantum Strike Tail, Last Stand Plate) use the highest %; "never wears off" (Permafrost at 5+, Rimeguard Plate) uses the lowest stack gate.
  • Rules that do add: combo ramps and falloff per strike (Chainlash Whip +10%, Endless Chain −15% → −5%); Afterburner Tail's "full damage" sets the base combo multiplier and falloff applies on top; the Quantum Guard Shell damage cap (20% max HP) applies last, after every damage-taken modifier (Mirror Carapace +20%).
  • Same effects stack (rev 3e): battle-start shields from all sources add up (Plasma Guard Helm + Frost Armor + Starguard + Bulwark Protocol); overheal-into-shield caps add up (Quantum Mend Helm 15% + Siphon Line 10% = up to 25% max HP); rage refunds add up (Plasma Fury Blaster + Pressure Valve); strike-backs on undone hits both fire (Hindsight Shell + Hour of Need); Drone guards from parts and the Hive Carrier each guard their own hit; on the Echo signature's 3rd turn Afterburner Tail's burst fires too, and rage from Momentum and the part adds up.
  • Blow-up caps (all sources together; decided by the owner): fully avoided hits (forced misses from Ashwalkers, Flicker Treads, Quantum Swift Legs and full sidekick guards) at most 1 per round, further ones fall back to normal dodge or the guard's own %; extra turns at most 2 per round (more move to the next round; an extra turn never grants another); free rage attacks at most 2 per turn; a status ticks at most 3 times per enemy per round (its own tick, Time Dilation, "ticks twice" and every "tick now"); Echoes chain at most 2 deep; Drones from all sources share one cap of 5; Supernova chains from Cinder Crown once (awakened twice) per turn.
  • Crowd control from parts stays once per enemy per battle, respects the status's own 3-round cooldown and never extends a running stun / freeze / skip; bosses lose 20 rage instead.
  • Rhythms are timers. A part's "every Nth turn / round" counts as a timer for kits that advance timers (Patience, Slipstream A5).

Overlaps kept on purpose

  • "Battle start: gain a shield…": stacks by rule: battle-start shields from all sources add up.
  • "Healing past full HP becomes a shield…": stacks by rule: overheal-shield caps from all sources add up.
  • "Rage attacks refund…": stacks by rule: rage refunds from all sources add up.
  • "Hits you undo are dealt…": stacks by rule: with Hour of Need both strike back (the full undone hit and 50% ATK).
  • "Drone guards…": stacks by rule: Drone guards from parts and the Hive Carrier each guard their own hit.
  • "times in a row…": stacks by rule: on the Echo signature's turn both fire, the burst turn is the big turn.
  • "combo strikes gives…": stacks by rule: rage from Momentum and this part adds up.
  • "combo strike deals…": opposite of Endless Chain's falloff; both are relative and add up per strike (+10% and -15% = -5%).
  • "counter chance against it…": same trigger as Mirror Carapace (first hit each round), different payload: a counter chance, not a reflect.
  • "Supernova triggers…": a relative threshold modifier; Chain Reaction refills Heat after a Supernova (different payload).
  • "Chill gains…": a Chill ramp; Fallout Aura adds Radiation per status (different status and condition).
  • "least Shock gains…": a Shock spreader; Fallout Aura adds Radiation per status (different status).
  • "Radiation die below…": executes never add up: the highest threshold of all sources applies (Cull 15-24% any hit, this one needs 30+/40+ Radiation).
  • "Crits on non-boss enemies below…": executes never add up: the highest threshold applies; this one needs a crit (Cull: any hit, lower threshold).
  • "first turn each battle ends with…": Opening Flurry buffs combo chance on the same turn; this adds an Echo.
  • "Rage attacks hit every enemy at…": Bloodletting adds a single-target hit; this turns the rage attack itself into an area attack.
  • "double your current shield…": grows an existing shield (capped) instead of granting one; Bulwark Protocol and Rebound Plating grant shields on counter.
  • "Arc jumps can crit…": a crit rule for Arc jumps; flat crit-chance skills raise every hit.
  • Thornstep Legs "first hit after a dodge applies 3 Bleed" and Serrated Edge "first hit each battle": same payload, different trigger (every dodge vs once per battle).
  • Dynamo Hull "when hit: Arc" and the Arc signature "on hit: Arc": the part answers hits you take, the signature hits you land.

Contradictions

  • Resolved: Deep Freeze resets Chill at 5, so "at 10 Chill" part skills could never fire: they are gated on "chilled" now. Capacitor resets Shock at its max of 5, so "at max Shock" never showed: 4+ / 3+ Shock now. Controlled Burn's 12-Heat Supernova and Cinder Crown's "8 instead of 10" could not both hold: relative now. Rage Turbine and the Quantum Fury Shell each set a rage cost: both relative, with one floor.
  • Kept as trade-offs of a run Mythic: Thermal Shock ("can't gain Heat for 3 turns") also blocks Heat from parts; Bastion (no attacks on your turn) silences "your attacks / basic attacks" part skills; Ice parts on a Fire build cool Heat unless the run has Cold Fusion. How rev 3 answers the review

  • "The base design of the parts is wrong." Parts are no longer one def climbing 14 rarities with five generated skills. Each rarity is its own set, standard sets fill plain roles, and only Mythics carry builds.
  • Inconsistent. One grid for all 120 standard items: role × slot × rarity, with fixed names (tier role slot: Scrap / Alloy / Plasma / Quantum + Guard … Fury + Helm / Shell / Tail / Legs / weapon). Every slot speaks one trigger language (head = openers and crits, body = when hit and low HP, tail = rhythm, legs = dodge and speed, arm = attacks: rage attacks, basic attacks, kills). Every Mythic has the same shape: five skills on the same ladder, each with its awakened version.
  • Duplication. A script compares every pair of the 390 skill texts (word overlap and a trigger + verb + status key) and every text against all run skills, signatures, hero and mount kits. Within a slot and rarity, no two skills share a mechanic. Standard part skills never use a synergy mechanic (no statuses, Arcs, Echoes, sidekicks or Supernovas), so they cannot duplicate a Mythic; their stats may carry combo, counter or crit chance where the role fits (owner, rev 3f). What is left is listed in section 5.
  • Identity not used. Every Mythic states one build identity and a keyword set; the script checks that each of its five skills and five awakened versions uses a keyword (all 300 do). Example: Ashwalkers is "dodge ignition", and every skill triggers on dodges or pays back into dodge.

Fixed during the pass

  • Rev 3b: the sixth (awakened) skill is gone from every Mythic; the 150 awakened versions were written per skill and checked against their base and every other text. Four new same-key clashes from the first draft were rewritten (Stormbore Drill and Worldbreaker Hammer rage-attack lines, Tempest Coil and Hyperburn Tail turn rhythms, Oracle Visor Echo lines).
  • Two Legendaries were word-for-word Mythic skills ("Rage attack kills refund 50 rage", "Crits apply Exposed"): the Mythic versions were replaced.
  • Three Legs Mythics opened with the same sentence ("On dodge: the attacker gains 2 Heat / Chill / Bleed"); only Ashwalkers keeps it, Frostglide Skates scales Chill on your dodge chance, Thornstep Legs loads Bleed on the first hit after a dodge.
  • Four area rage attacks (Firebrand Blaster, Shatter Cannon, Backlash Hammer, Rage Turbine) cut to one (Rage Turbine, the General rage engine); Shatter Cannon carried two crowd-control skills, now one.
  • Too close to existing kits or run skills: Afterburner Tail "every 3rd turn +combo chance" (Noodle Knot), Feint Mask "attacker deals less after a counter" (Brace), Last Stand Plate "fatal hit leaves 1 HP" (Last Word / Borrowed Sand), Hindsight Shell "undone hits strike back" (Hour of Need), Hatchery Tail Drone reload (Launch Bay): all replaced.
  • Crit-damage ramps on four items (Deadeye Visor, two Keen Legendaries, Foresight Visor Echo ramp) cut to one ramp per stat.

Remaining intentional overlaps

  • Deliberate families in the standard sets. The role sets the payload, the slot sets the trigger, so e.g. the Guard Epics all give shields (battle start, every 3rd turn, on dodge, on rage attack) and the Swift items all ramp speed or tempo. Distinct within each slot and rarity.
  • Identity loops inside one Mythic (flagged by the key, kept on purpose): Rampart Carapace counters → shield and shield hit → counter.
  • Two dodge Mythics with crowd control (flagged by the key, kept): Frostglide Skates freezes the most chilled enemy on every 3rd dodge, Timeslip Boots makes the attacker lose a turn with a 25% chance; the script's key also pairs Ashwalkers' no-recoil rule with Frostglide's dodge cap (different effects).
  • Same payload, different build: Ashwalkers (Supernova → dodge) and Flicker Treads (combo strike → dodge); Firebrand Blaster (Supernova → another rage attack) and Backlash Hammer (every 3rd counter → free rage attack); sure crits on Deadeye Visor (every 4th hit) and Epic / Legendary Keen items (first hits, every 3rd turn).
  • Shared vocabulary with run skills (same idea, different trigger or scope, never the same text): Legendary overheal → shield (Siphon Line, Second Wind), Quantum Swift Tail extra turn every 5th turn (Borrowed Time is every round with a cost), act first from round 3 (Slipstream is round 1), Deadeye Visor crit execute below 30% (Cull is any hit below 15%), Dynamo Hull when-hit Arc (Arc signature is on hit).
